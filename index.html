      Questions
      
      
      What providers ask before we start.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
        Do we have to replace the systems we use now?
        
      No. We build inside Google Workspace, Excel and the EHR exports you already have. Most tools sit alongside your current systems and take work off them rather than replacing them.
      
      
      
      
      
        Do our staff need technical skills?
        
      No. Portals open from a link. Python tools arrive as ordinary Windows programs with buttons and file pickers, so nobody opens a terminal. Settings live inside each tool's admin screen, not in code.
      
      
      
      
      
        How long does a project take?
        
      Engagements are short by design. We scope a complete first delivery around one bottleneck, ship it, and build from there. You'll know the timeline for your tool before any work starts.
      
      
      
      
      
        How is our data handled?
        
      Tools run in accounts your organization controls, with role-based access and audit trails. Where AI reads a document, a person approves the result before it's saved. We walk through the data flow for your specific tool before anything is built.
      
      
      
      
      
        Do you only work with disability and behavioral health providers?
        
      That's where our depth is and where most of our work happens. If your operation has the same kind of drag, like documents, reconciliation or compliance deadlines, tell us about it.
      
      
      
      
      
        What if our process can't be automated?
        
      Then we'll tell you. Some work needs human judgment. Our job is to clear away everything around it so that judgment gets more of your team's time.
